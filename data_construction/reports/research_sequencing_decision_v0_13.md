## What changed

The new design and its output-absent Git receipt freeze the next diagnostic's
input boundary, stage order, three synthetic controls, one-launch-per-case
limit, stopping branches and evidence limitations. The freezer binds the
failed run's twenty files, dispatch receipt, old contracts/runtime/tests and
exposure manifests byte-for-byte. It does not inspect binding content to tune
semantic criteria. No transport adapter, platform probe, clean author context,
new research response, execution or answer has been produced by this design.

The contract is coordinator-side metadata, not an author packet. Do not send
this report, its failure summary or the design's source inventory to a probe
author. A separately frozen neutral probe protocol must be projected from the
operational requirements without research facts or question IDs.

## Evidence motivating the design

The v0.1 dispatch requested non-forked contexts but all six raw responses
reported excluded research summaries in automatically supplied project
instructions. This establishes an observed failure, not a complete inventory
of privileged platform inputs. The preserved source of this observation is
sequencing decision v0.12 and its hash-bound dispatch/raw artifacts.

Codex documentation says instruction discovery combines global guidance and
project-path guidance at startup. A new session therefore is not evidence
that no project guidance was supplied. This design choice was informed by
the OpenAI Docs skill and the official
[AGENTS.md discovery documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/agents-md).
The documentation explains discovery; it does not certify the exact hosted
channel used here or promise cognitive independence.

## Two distinct evidence sources

1. A future host adapter must capture the delivered input assembly, not just
   the intended packet allowlist. Inventory all nine context-source classes,
   record available identities and capture limitations, and bind the ordered
   input manifest, configuration, context ID, nonce and event log.
2. An author may report known, unknown or no known prior exposure. A known
   violation stops the context; an unknown blocks it. A clean self-report is
   insufficient to fill missing host evidence.

Do not ask authors or tools to reveal hidden prompts, reasoning or secrets.
Opaque platform policy can be described by available identity and independently
reviewable data-flow boundaries rather than disclosed text. If the possible
injection of excluded project/history information cannot be ruled out at that
boundary, remain blocked. Hashes prove saved byte identity, not that a host
inventory is complete or truthful. No local validator can authenticate latent
inference or model pretraining exposure.

## Precommitted controls and transitions

The question-free suite has three cases, with at most one actual launch per
case after the adapter and exact probe plan are separately frozen:

| Synthetic case | Required per-context outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Closed, fully evidenced neutral context | `QUALIFIED_FOR_SEPARATE_PLAN_ONLY` |
| Deliberately injected synthetic excluded marker | `STOP_INPUT_ISOLATION_FAILURE` |
| Deliberately opaque input inventory | `BLOCKED_UNVERIFIABLE_CONTEXT` |

An expected STOP/BLOCK is a control success, not clearance of that context.
Unexpected control outcomes stop the suite; missing captures are incomplete;
an unverifiable closed-context case blocks qualification. No known-marker
detection rate establishes comprehensive isolation. The future adapter must
independently substantiate the approved host boundary as well.

Freeze every first capture, including failures; no selective retries or
repair. A configuration, transport or protocol change needs a new version.
Do not reuse clearance after a context, history, tool or input change. Even a
qualified adapter does not authorize research: a separate grounding plan and
per-context pre-packet admission plus post-delivery audit remain required.

Protocol, author-guide and response-schema hashes will refer to their own
named byte streams in a future packet version. The old `protocol_sha256`
author-guide alias is preserved unchanged, not treated as source corruption.
